# Blocking and hiding profiles

## Block a profile {#block}

1. Open the profile.
2. Tap **Block** and confirm.

In the grid you can also long-press a profile and block it from the quick actions.

To unblock, open the profile and tap **Unblock**, or go to **Settings → Blocked Accounts**.

You can turn off the confirmation prompt for blocking and unblocking in **Settings → Behavior**.

## Hide a profile {#hide}

1. Open the profile.
2. Tap **Hide from grid**.

Or long-press the profile in the grid and hide it from the quick actions.

To unhide, open the profile and tap **Unhide from grid**, or go to **Settings → Hidden Profiles**. There you can also unhide everyone at once.

## What's the difference? {#difference}

|                                   | Block                         | Hide from grid                                |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| They disappear from your grid     | Yes                           | Yes                                           |
| They can still see and message you | No                            | Yes                                           |
| Conversation                      | Removed for both of you       | Kept                                          |
| Undo                              | **Settings → Blocked Accounts** | **Settings → Hidden Profiles**              |

## Who blocked me? {#block-activity}

**Settings → Block Activity** shows who has blocked or unblocked you, and when.

## Block automatically {#auto-block}

**Settings → Automation** can block senders of new chats automatically, for example everyone outside an age range or anyone whose first message contains a forbidden keyword. You can also build your own rules that check the sender of a first message and then block, reply or share an album.
