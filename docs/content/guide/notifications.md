# Notifications

Free Grind can notify you about:

- **New messages**: every message shows up in the notification, grouped per chat, with buttons to jump straight into the chat or the profile.
- **Received taps**

Choose which ones you get in **Settings → Notifications**. There you can also turn off **Notifications While App Is Open** if you only want to be notified while Free Grind is in the background or closed.

## Android

Notifications arrive instantly through Firebase Cloud Messaging, even when Free Grind is closed. This needs Google Play services on the device.

To change the alert level, sound, or whether the text appears on the lock screen, use Android's system settings for Free Grind.

## iOS and iPadOS

Notifications are shown while Free Grind is running. To change banners, sounds or previews, go to iOS **Settings → Notifications → Free Grind**.

## Windows

Notifications only arrive while Free Grind is running.

To change banners, sound or lock screen previews, go to **Settings → System → Notifications → Free Grind**.

## macOS

Notifications only arrive while Free Grind is running.

To change the alert style, sound or previews, go to **System Settings → Notifications → Free Grind**.

## Linux

Free Grind shows notifications through your desktop's notification service. GNOME, KDE Plasma and most other desktops work out of the box. On a standalone window manager, install a notification service such as [dunst](https://dunst-project.org/) or [mako](https://github.com/emersion/mako).

Notifications only arrive while Free Grind is running. To change how they look or sound, use your desktop's notification settings.
