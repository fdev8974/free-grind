# No ads

The official Grindr app puts ads in the grid, in chats, on profiles and between almost every action, and keeps pushing you to upgrade.

Free Grind shows none of that: no ads, no promotions, no banners, no upsells. It's free, open source and has no premium version. If someone asks you to pay for it, it's a scam.
