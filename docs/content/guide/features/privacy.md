# Privacy

The official Grindr app is full of trackers and analytics. Free Grind has none: it talks to Grindr directly, doesn't run any server of its own, and nothing you do in the app is reported anywhere else.

On top of that, **Settings → Privacy** gives you control over what other people see:

- **Incognito Mode:** the grid doesn't load or refresh, read receipts are never sent, and profile visits don't register as views. You can use the app without showing up as online or leaving a trace.
- **Send Read Receipts:** turn off to read messages without the other person knowing until you reply. With **Per-Chat Overrides**, the eye button in the chat menu changes this for single chats.
- **Record Profile Views:** turn off to look at profiles without showing up in their viewers list.
- **Blur Profile Pictures in Grid:** for browsing discreetly in public.
- **Sensitive Content Warning:** a warning screen before opening an album that could contain sensitive content.
- **App Disguise** (Android, iOS): swaps the app's icon and name on your home screen for a decoy (Calculator, Notes or Weather).

## Network requests

Besides Grindr, Free Grind only contacts GitHub to check for updates. Turn off **Check Automatically** in **Settings** to stop that, and check manually instead. Translation and GIFs contact their providers only when you use them.

## Account safety

- Using any unofficial client technically violates [Grindr's terms of service](https://www.grindr.com/terms-of-service). The risk is low, but it exists.
- Never share sign-in codes from **Sign in on another device**. Anyone with the code can sign in to your account.
- Only download Free Grind from the [official releases page](https://github.com/fdev8974/free-grind/releases).
