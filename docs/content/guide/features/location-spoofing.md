# Location spoofing

Free Grind lets you set your Grindr location anywhere in the world, without a subscription and without faking your device's GPS. Grindr sees the location you pick as if you were there: your grid loads from that spot, and people there see your profile nearby.

## Teleport

1. On **Browse**, open the location picker.
2. Search for a place, or tap anywhere on the map. On mobile you can also use your GPS position.
3. Confirm. Your grid reloads from the new location.

## Saved locations

Tap the bookmark in the location picker to save the pinned spot. Saved locations show up in the picker for quick switching, and you can share them in a chat.

## Travel plans

In **Settings → Profile Editor** you can add trips, so others see where you're headed and when while the trip is active.
