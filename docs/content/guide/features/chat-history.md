# Local chat history

Free Grind keeps a copy of your conversations on your device. Messages and received media stay available even when Grindr no longer returns them, for example after an expiring photo, an unsent message or a block.

## What it gives you

- **Chat search** across all your conversations.
- **Profiles that are gone:** if someone's live profile isn't available any more, Free Grind shows the last info saved on your device.
- **Block activity:** **Settings → Block Activity** shows who has blocked or unblocked you, and when.
- **Video gallery:** every video you've received in chats and albums in one place. Turn it on in **Settings → Optional Features**.
- **Revealed viewers:** anonymous profile viewers are matched with your history, see [Views and taps](/guide/features/interest).

## Backup and restore {#backup}

**Backup & Restore** in **Settings** saves your data to a file and restores it on another device or after a reinstall. You choose what to include:

- Chat messages
- Media and albums
- Saved phrases
- Saved locations
- Settings
- Sexual health data

On Android you can save the backup to any folder or cloud storage, for example Google Drive.

**Settings → Export Chat Data** additionally downloads all locally stored messages as a JSON file.

::: warning
Uninstalling Free Grind deletes the local history. Make a backup first if you want to keep it.
:::
