# Unlocked grid

The official Grindr app only lets free users scroll through a small part of the grid before asking for a subscription. Free Grind keeps loading profiles page after page, so you can browse the whole grid for free.

## Browse faster

- **Double tap** a profile to send a tap, or to open the chat directly, without opening the profile. Choose what double tap does in **Settings → Behavior**.
- **Long press** a profile for quick actions: message, favorite, set a nickname, hide or block.
- **Search by name:** filter the grid by display name.
- **Filters:** choose who appears in your grid by age, height, weight or tags, use quick filters, and change the sort order.
- **2, 3 or 4 columns** on mobile, set in **Settings → Look & Feel**.
- A small flame marks profiles you've tapped or who tapped you in the last 24 hours.

## Browse anywhere

The grid loads from whatever location you choose, anywhere in the world. See [Location spoofing](/guide/features/location-spoofing).

## Browse invisibly

With **Incognito Mode** on, the grid doesn't load at all, so you never show up as online. See [Privacy](/guide/features/privacy).
