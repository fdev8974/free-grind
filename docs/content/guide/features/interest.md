# Views and taps

The **Interest** tab shows who viewed your profile and who tapped you, plus the taps you sent.

## See more of your viewers

Grindr only shows the very last visitor in full to free users. The others are hidden behind a paywall or shown as anonymous previews.

Free Grind matches these anonymous visitors with your [local chat history](/guide/features/chat-history). If it finds a match, the profile is revealed to you.

## Taps

The **Taps** list shows everyone who tapped you, and their profile shows which kind of tap they sent. Taps you sent are listed separately, with a mark once the recipient has seen them.

To send a tap straight from the grid, double tap a profile. See [Unlocked grid](/guide/features/unlocked-grid).
