# Multiple accounts

## Switch between accounts

Add more Grindr accounts with **Add Profile** and switch between them without signing out. Each account keeps its own chats and unread counts.

## Move to a new device

Sign an account in on another device without typing your password:

1. On the signed-in device, open **Settings** and tap **Sign in on another device**.
2. On the new device, tap **Signed in on another device? Use a QR code** on the sign-in screen and scan the code, or paste it.

See [Sign in](/guide/login#sign-in-on-another-device-qr-code).

## Several windows on Windows

On Windows, Free Grind can run several instances side by side, each with its own account and data.
