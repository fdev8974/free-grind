# Sexual health

Free Grind has a built-in tracker for your sexual health. Everything stays on your device and is never sent to Grindr. Turn it on in **Settings → Optional Features**.

- **PrEP:** log your doses and see your protection status, for daily or on-demand (2-1-1) PrEP.
- **Encounters:** log who you hooked up with and when. In a chat, use **We just hooked up** in the chat menu.
- **Tests:** keep track of your STI tests over time.
- **Appointments:** add follow-up or screening appointments.

In **Settings → Profile Editor** you can also turn on a reminder to get tested when it's been a while since your last test.

Include sexual health data in a [backup](/guide/features/chat-history#backup) to move it to another device.
